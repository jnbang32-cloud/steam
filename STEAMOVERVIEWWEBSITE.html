This is a general overview of me (Joon Bang), Jupiter Choi, and Jay's steam project Our topic: How can the oobleck ratio affect the sinking time when a metal sphere (8.96g) is dropped from 1m? Hypothesis: A water-to-cornstarch ratio of 1:2 produces the highest viscosity, resulting in the longest time for a metal object to submerge when dropped from a height of 1 m into the oobleck. Background: Oobleck is a non-Newtonian fluid that behaves like a solid when sufficient force is applied quickly. It is made from a mixture of cornstarch and water. Because the cornstarch does not fully dissolve and remains suspended in the wate, applying force causes the particles to jam together, making the mixture act like a solid. When at rest the water lets the cornstartch float and acts like a liquid. When forced is added the water is forced out between the cornstartch, temporarily acting like a solid. In this investigation, we will test water-to-cornstarch ratios of 1:1*, 1:1.5, 2:1, 2:2.5, and 2:3. We aim to determine whether the ratio of water to cornstarch affects the time it takes for the oobleck to return to its liquid state after force is applied.
